Stage 0: Decode Theme Like a Boss

Learn to crush the difference between topic, main idea, and theme.

🎬 What's the Difference?

TOPIC = The SUBJECT (one or two words)

Example: FRIENDSHIP

MAIN IDEA = What HAPPENS in the story (summary of plot)

Example: A friend breaks a promise and loses trust

THEME = The BIG MESSAGE about life (what it MEANS)

Example: Trust is fragile and must be earned through consistent action

🔥 Remember: Theme is a COMPLETE SENTENCE that answers "What truth about life or human nature does this story show?" A single word is TOPIC. A plot summary is MAIN IDEA.

Stage 4: Write Your Essay

Compose a multi-paragraph essay analyzing your story's theme.

Essay Structure

  • Introduction: Introduce the story and state your theme clearly.
  • Body Paragraph 1: Analyze Scene 1 and its connection to the theme.
  • Body Paragraph 2: Analyze Scene 2 and its connection to the theme.
  • Body Paragraph 3: Examine the character's challenge and choice; explain how it supports your theme.
  • Conclusion: Reflect on what the theme reveals about human nature or relationships.
